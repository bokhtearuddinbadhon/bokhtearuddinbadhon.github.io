1. Problem Framing & Socio-Environmental Context

In urban areas such as Ladywood, Birmingham, aging and poorly insulated housing stock leads to severe heat loss and excessive fuel expenses. Conventional passive wall vents allow warm air to escape continually during cold months, forcing households to choose between living in cold, under-heated conditions or suffering indoor dampness, condensation, and black mould growth.

The Aerovent was engineered as a decentralised, non-invasive retrofit heat recovery ventilator (dHRV). Sized to fit standard 100 mm to 125 mm residential wall core apertures, it delivers constant air renewal without the substantial thermal losses of passive ventilation.

2. Thermal-Fluids Principles & Regenerative Cycle

The unit operates on an alternating regenerative thermodynamic exchange cycle:

  1. Exhaust Stroke (70 seconds): A low-voltage axial fan extracts warm, stale indoor air (T_indoor ≈ 20°C). As it passes through the ceramic honeycomb matrix, heat is absorbed and stored due to the ceramic material's high volumetric heat capacity.
  2. Intake Stroke (70 seconds): The fan reverses direction to draw in cold, fresh outdoor air (T_outdoor ≈ 2°C to 5°C). The cold intake air absorbs the thermal energy stored in the ceramic core, warming up to near indoor temperatures before discharging into the room.
Thermal Heat Transfer Rate: Q = m_dot * Cp * ΔT
Heat Exchanger Temperature Efficiency: η_thermal = (T_supply - T_outdoor) / (T_exhaust - T_outdoor) * 100%

By utilising a ceramic porous honeycomb core, the system achieves a substantial contact surface area within a compact Ø100 mm × 200 mm tube profile, maintaining low air resistance and low electrical power consumption.

4. Mechanical Architecture & Maintenance Engineering

  • Cylindrical Housing: Lightweight uPVC outer sleeve designed for standard UK core-drilled wall sleeves with an integrated drip edge on the exterior hood.
  • Regenerative Matrix: Cordierite ceramic porous honeycomb core providing high thermal storage density and chemical stability against condensation.
  • Serviceable Filter Assembly: Washable G3/G4 particulate air filters and exterior insect screens located in easily removable end caps for routine resident maintenance.
  • Acoustic Isolation: Sound-dampening foam lining surrounding the reversible DC fan motor to maintain acoustic comfort in residential living areas.
