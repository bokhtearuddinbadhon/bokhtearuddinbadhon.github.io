1. Project Scope & Engineering Workflow

As mechanical systems increasingly interface with mechatronic actuators, sensors, and electronic feedback controllers, practical fluency in circuit instrumentation and network modeling is essential. This rigorous five-experiment portfolio systematically progressed from steady-state DC verification to dynamic alternating-current oscilloscope measurements.

  1. Ohm's Law & Power Law: Characterized DC power dissipation curves ($P = V^2/R$) across standard E12 resistors and evaluated loading in voltage and current divider circuits.
  2. Kirchhoff's Laws (KVL & KCL): Constructed multi-loop planar bridge networks, evaluated mesh residual voltages, and verified conservation of current at key junctions.
  3. Thévenin & Norton Source Transformation: Simplified a complex multi-node bridge into single-source equivalents ($V_{Th} = 10.720\text{ V}, R_{Th} \approx 2.972\text{ k}\Omega, I_N = 3.608\text{ mA}$) and evaluated terminal load response.
  4. AC Waveform Analysis: Employed high-speed DSO triggering and calibrated cursors to quantify peak-to-peak voltages, frequency errors ($1.86\%$), and RMS correlation against true-RMS bench multimeters ($0.84\%$).
  5. Impedance & Magnetic Permeability: Integrated a $1\,\Omega$ non-inductive shunt resistor (NIR) to isolate current waveforms, measuring a $+193\%$ inductance increase when inserting a high-permeability ferrite rod.

2. Governing Mathematical Models

Ohm's Law & DC Power: V = I \cdot R \quad \text{and} \quad P = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}
Kirchhoff's Laws: \sum V_{\text{loop}} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \sum I_{\text{node}} = 0
Thévenin & Norton Equivalence: R_{Th} = \frac{V_{o/c}}{I_{s/c}}, \quad I_L = \frac{V_{Th}}{R_{Th} + R_L}
AC Impedance & Reactance: |Z| = \frac{V_{\text{coil}}}{I}, \quad X_L = |Z|\sin(\phi), \quad L = \frac{X_L}{2\pi f}
Magnetic Circuit Reluctance: L = \frac{N^2}{\mathcal{R}} \quad \text{where} \quad \mathcal{R} = \frac{l}{\mu A}

4. Engineering Diagnostics & Placement Reflection

  • Instrument Loading Effects: Confirmed why high-impedance ($10\text{ M}\Omega$) voltmeters are mandatory when measuring divider networks; low input impedance shunts current and loads down sensitive nodal voltages.
  • Ammeter Burden Resistance: In the current divider experiment, observed branch currents slightly lower than theoretical ($3.29\text{ mA}$ vs $3.33\text{ mA}$) due to the internal sensing resistor of the DMM in series.
  • Phase Displacement Mechanics: Directly visualized inductive current lagging applied voltage by $48.4^\circ$ on the DSO. In pure resistance, the phase was verified at $0.0^\circ$, proving energy dissipation without phase lag.
  • Critical Evaluation of Outliers: In Experiment 3, a large deviation in the $4.7\text{ k}\Omega$ load comparison demonstrated that real-world engineering requires rigorous diagnosis of breadboard wiring and resistor color-code reading rather than hiding anomalies.
