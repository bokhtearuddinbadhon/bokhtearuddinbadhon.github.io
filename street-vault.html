1. Urban Challenge & Problem Framing

London's historic street network was constructed long before mass automobile adoption[cite: 30]. Today, parked roadside vehicles reduce effective carriageways to single-lane bottlenecks, impede London Fire Brigade and NHS emergency vehicle access, force pedestrians and prams onto roads, and restrict lateral clearance for cyclists[cite: 30, 31, 32].

Controlled Parking Zones (CPZs) and permits redistribute cars without creating physical surface space[cite: 30, 33]. StreetVault resolves this by moving static vehicle storage vertically below ground level, leaving the upper road deck flush and operational for public transit when closed[cite: 30, 33, 38].

2. Mechanical & Hydraulic System Principles

Actuation Principle: Hydraulic fluid power governs movement based on Pascal's Law (pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished in all directions)[cite: 35, 57]. A high lifting force at four synchronized corner cylinders is generated using a compact electric-motor-driven pump unit[cite: 35, 36, 57].

The mechanical drive train follows an integrated sequence: Electric Motor → Hydraulic Pump → Directional Control Valves → Synchronized Cylinders → Rigid Lifting Platform[cite: 35, 36, 57].

  • Dynamic Lifting Capacity: Sized for private passenger cars (1,200 kg to 2,500 kg) with safety factor redundancy[cite: 35, 36].
  • Structural Integrity: Reinforced waterproof concrete vault designed to resist lateral soil pressure, hydrostatic groundwater loads, and heavy overhead highway traffic[cite: 34].
  • CAD Iteration: Assembly designed and spatially verified using Autodesk Fusion 360 to align platform clearances with standard UK parking stall footprints[cite: 49, 56].

3. Controlled Operational Workflow

  1. Request Initiated: Driver triggers parking request via user interface or credential scan[cite: 37, 38, 58].
  2. Zone Clearance & Isolation: Optical and traffic sensors confirm clear street space; perimeter barriers and warning alerts activate[cite: 38, 58].
  3. Hydraulic Elevation: Synchronized hydraulic cylinders smoothly elevate the platform flush to street grade[cite: 38, 58].
  4. Positioning & Alignment: Driver positions vehicle; integrated weight and limit switches verify alignment[cite: 38, 58].
  5. Descent & Vault Locking: Platform descends into the reinforced underground vault and mechanical locking pins engage[cite: 37, 38, 58].
  6. Surface Resumption: Flush heavy-duty top deck re-opens for unrestricted pedestrian and vehicular roadway passage[cite: 38, 58].

5. Risk Mitigation & Safety Engineering

  • Hydraulic Failure & Drift: Redundant synchronized cylinders, fail-safe pilot-operated check valves, mechanical lock pins, and emergency manual lowering overrides[cite: 36, 37, 44, 59].
  • Water Infiltration: Sub-grade waterproof membrane, perimeter channel drains, and dual automatic sump pumps with battery backup[cite: 34, 44, 59].
  • Ground Settlement: Reinforced footing design conforming to UK Building Regulations and British civil standards following geotechnical site investigation[cite: 44, 59].
