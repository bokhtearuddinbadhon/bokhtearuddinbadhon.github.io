Universal Testing Machine (UTM) Experimental Recording

Dogbone specimen elongation, crosshead displacement & fracture
Institution & Lab London South Bank University | Solid Mechanics
Testing System Universal Testing Machine (UTM) & Extensometer
Specimen Geometry Dogbone: L0 = 50 mm, W = 12 mm, T = 6 mm (A0 = 72 mm²)
Crosshead Speed 2 mm/min (Quasi-Static Uniaxial Loading)

Experimental Workflow & Testing Methodology

  1. Specimen Metrology & Inspection: Visually checked dogbone specimen for machining marks, surface flaws, or oxidation. Recorded critical dimensions using digital vernier calipers (±0.02 mm tolerance).
  2. Grip Alignment & Hydraulic Clamping: Mounted specimen securely into higher and lower hydraulic grips, verifying vertical alignment marks to eliminate parasitic bending moments.
  3. Extensometer Calibration: Attached a high-precision clip-on extensometer (±5 microstrain accuracy) across the 50 mm gauge marks and zeroed both load cell and displacement sensors.
  4. Quasi-Static Displacement Application: Applied controlled tensile displacement at a steady crosshead rate of 2 mm/min, logging real-time force and extension coordinates.
  5. Elastic-to-Plastic Transition: Monitored Hookean linear elasticity and the distinct upper/lower yield points, removing the extensometer before significant plastic elongation.
  6. Strain Hardening & Necking: Observed dislocation accumulation driving plastic flow until Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS), followed by localized geometric necking.
  7. Ductile Fracture Analysis: Recorded final separation featuring characteristic cup-and-cone morphology, confirming high ductility and predictable structural warning before failure.
