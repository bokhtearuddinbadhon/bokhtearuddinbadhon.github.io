Computational Architecture & Methodology

  1. Engineering Physics to Executable Code: Translated governing thermodynamic and mechanics relationships (e.g. sensible heat energy Q = mcΔT) into structured, unit-controlled C++ algorithms.
  2. Defensive Logic & Input Validation: Guarded critical mathematical operations against runtime failure (such as division by zero and out-of-range bounds) using robust relational and boolean operators.
  3. Numerical Series & Iterative Convergence: Implemented mathematical series for π approximation, tracking error thresholds and convergence rates across successive iterations.
  4. Stochastic Modeling (Monte Carlo): Formulated random-sampling simulation models (e.g. virtual dart and frequency distribution experiments) to quantify probability and event occurrences.
  5. Spatial Coordinate Geometry & 2D Arrays: Applied Euclidean distance formulations to multi-dimensional coordinate arrays to find the minimum distance between pairs of spatial points.
  6. Data Pipelines & CSV Export: Streamed generated numerical datasets (e.g. trigonometric cosine sweeps) into external CSV files via std::ofstream for post-processing and graphing.
  7. Object-Oriented Design & Polymorphism: Structured modular systems using classes, encapsulation, constructors, inheritance, and virtual functions to model multi-state mechanical and banking logic.
