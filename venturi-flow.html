Governing Fluid Mechanics & Theoretical Model

As an incompressible fluid traverses a constriction, continuity enforces velocity acceleration with a corresponding static pressure drop according to Bernoulli's principle. Accounting for the velocity-of-approach factor:

Continuity: Q = A1 × v1 = A2 × v2
Ideal Bernoulli Head: H = z + (p / (ρg)) + (v² / (2g)) = constant
Theoretical Volumetric Flow Rate: Q_th = A2 × √[(2g Δh) / (1 - (A2/A1)²)]
Discharge Coefficient: Cd = Q_actual / Q_theoretical

Comparative Engineering Evaluation

Design Parameter Venturi Meter (Converging-Diverging) Orifice Plate (Sharp-Edged Constriction)
Discharge Coefficient High (Average Cd ≈ 0.962); matches ideal flow model. Lower (Average Cd ≈ 0.610); substantial flow contraction.
Fluid Dynamics Streamlined gradual expansion minimizes boundary layer separation. Sudden area contraction forms a vena contracta and high wake turbulence.
Permanent Head Loss Low permanent energy dissipation; optimal for continuous pumping systems. High irreversible pressure loss; higher parasitic pumping power costs.
Practical Installation Bulky, longer axial footprint, higher upfront capital manufacturing cost. Compact, low installation cost, clamped easily between pipe flanges.

Measurement Uncertainty & Experimental Control

  • Hydraulic Calibration: Checked zero datum on multi-tube manometer tubes and purged entrained air bubbles before recording.
  • Timing Error Mitigation: Gravimetric mass collection (6 kg) over extended intervals (12.24 s to 25.42 s) mitigated manual stopwatch human reaction time error (±0.2 s).
  • Parallax Control: Manometer piezometric head levels were read at eye-level against the metric back-plate to minimize optical refraction through the water column.
