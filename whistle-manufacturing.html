Process Sequence & Methodology

  1. Stock Preparation: Inspected ~90 mm raw aluminium 6082-T6 bar stock, faced the datum reference plane, and centered the piece in a 3-jaw chuck.
  2. Parallel External Turning: Turned the outer diameter to the specified 11.00 mm dimension with continuous digital micrometer checks.
  3. Axial Center Drilling & Boring: Step-drilled the central acoustic bore to 42.00 mm depth, maintaining concentricity along the central axis.
  4. Form Grooving: Formed exterior acoustic grip rings at 3–6 mm, 22–23 mm, 25–26 mm, and 28–29 mm positions.
  5. Vertical Milling: Clamped the cylinder in a dedicated milling vice jig; milled the flat surface and the V-shaped acoustic air escape slot.
  6. Fipple Fitting, Deburring & Metrology: Hand-filed edges, press-fitted the internal acoustic fipple plug, and verified critical tolerances.
