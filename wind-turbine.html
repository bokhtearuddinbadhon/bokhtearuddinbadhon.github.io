1. Investigation Overview & Research Aims

A controlled experimental investigation completed on the Armfield RE12 test platform comparing the energy conversion capabilities, aerodynamic starting behaviours, and wake velocity deceleration between a 3-blade lift-based HAWT and a drag-based Savonius VAWT across 8 excitation settings (5.0 V to 12.0 V).

2. Aerodynamic Laws & Governing Calculations

Empirical measurements conformed strictly to theoretical fluid dynamics principles:

Available Kinetic Wind Power: Pw = 0.5 × ρ × A × Vw³
Terminal Electrical Power: Pr = U × I
Power Extraction Coefficient: Cp = Pr / Pw
Betz Limit Theoretical Benchmark: P_Betz = 0.593 × Pw

Cubic Velocity Verification: Raising the upstream velocity from 2.51 m/s to 6.00 m/s (2.39× increase) resulted in an exact 13.65× available power growth (conforming directly to (2.39)³ = 13.65).
